Fruit Salad

Description

This sweet, rich dessert uses canned fruit cocktail, coconut gels, and a heavy cream-and-condensed-milk dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (836g / 30 oz) fruit cocktail, thoroughly drained of syrup
  • 1 bottle (12 oz) nata de coco (coconut gel), drained
  • 1 bottle (12 oz) kaong (sugar palm fruit), drained
  • 1 lb fresh coconut strips (buko)
  • 4 oz cheddar or processed cheese (e.g., Eden), cut into tiny cubes (optional)
  • 1 pack (250 ml) all-purpose cream (chilled)
  • 1 can (14 oz or ~300-390 ml) sweetened condensed milk
  • ½ cup raisins (optional)

Steps

  1. Drain the fruits: Use a colander or strainer to thoroughly drain the syrup from the fruit cocktail, nata de coco, and kaong for at least 30 minutes to prevent a watery salad.
  2. Mix the cream base: In a large mixing bowl, combine the chilled all-purpose cream and sweetened condensed milk. Stir well until smoothly blended.
  3. Combine ingredients: Add the drained fruit cocktail, nata de coco, kaong, coconut strips, cubed cheese, and raisins into the cream mixture.
  4. Fold gently: Use a spatula or spoon to gently fold everything together until all the fruits are coated.
  5. Chill and serve: Cover the bowl with cling wrap and refrigerate for at least 3 to 4 hours (or overnight) to let the flavors meld. Serve cold.
