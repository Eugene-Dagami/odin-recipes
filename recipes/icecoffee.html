Ice Coffee

Freshly brewed coffee chilled and served over ice—smooth, refreshing,
and easy to customize with milk or sweetener.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup freshly brewed coffee (cooled to room temperature) — OR 2 tsp instant coffee dissolved in ½ cup hot water
  • 1 to 2 tbsp sugar, brown sugar, or simple syrup (adjust to taste)
  • ¼ to ½ cup cold milk (fresh milk, oat milk, or evaporated milk)
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • Optional flavor boosters: ½ tsp vanilla extract, a dash of cinnamon, or 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk (for a sweet, creamy Spanish-style iced coffee)

Steps

  1. Brew and sweeten: Brew your coffee (or dissolve the instant coffee in hot water). Stir in your sugar or sweetener while the coffee is still warm so it dissolves completely.
  2. Cool it down: Let the sweetened coffee cool down for a few minutes so it doesn't instantly melt all the ice.
  3. Assemble: Fill a tall glass to the top with ice cubes.
  4. Pour the coffee: Pour the cooled coffee over the ice, leaving about an inch or two of space at the top.
  5. Add milk and stir: Pour in your cold milk (and vanilla extract, if using), stir well, and serve immediately.
